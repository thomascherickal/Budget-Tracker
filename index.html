Platform Storage Permissions Guide

Sovereign Budget is 100% offline-first. Your financial data is saved strictly on your device. Below is how to ensure unrestricted local storage permission on your platform:

Linux (Zorin OS 17/18, Ubuntu, Debian, Fedora)

When launched via direct file:/// protocol, Chromium-based browsers may restrict storage. Use our one-click zero-dependency runner or launcher:

./start.sh

Or run an instant native local server via Python or Node:

python3 -m http.server 8000
node serve.js

Windows (10 / 11) - Chrome, Edge & Firefox

Double-click start.bat in the repository folder, or in Edge/Chrome:

  • Open Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies and site data.
  • Ensure "Allow sites to save and read cookie data" is turned ON.
  • Ensure "Block third-party cookies" does not block localhost/file origin.

macOS (Safari & Chrome)

  • Safari: In Safari Preferences > Advanced, enable "Show Develop menu in menu bar". In the Develop menu, verify "Disable Local File Restrictions" if opening via file://.
  • Or run ./start.sh in Terminal to open unrestricted via local host.

Android (Chrome, Samsung Internet, Firefox)

  • Open browser Settings > Site settings > Storage / Device storage > set to Allowed.
  • PWA Installation: Tap menu (three dots) > "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen" for dedicated, persistent sandboxed storage.

iOS & iPadOS (Safari)

  • Open device Settings > Safari > Advanced: Ensure "Block All Cookies" is OFF.
  • Avoid Private Browsing if persistent multi-day local caching is needed, or tap Share > "Add to Home Screen" to run as a standalone PWA.
